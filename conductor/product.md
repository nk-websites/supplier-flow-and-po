# Product Definition — NewKommerce

## Vision

NewKommerce is the operational back office a cloud-kitchen / food-delivery
business uses to manage its **suppliers**, **raw-material products**,
**inventory movements**, and **purchase orders**. Kitchen managers and
procurement staff use it daily to know what is in stock, what needs
reordering, and which vendors to buy from.

## Users

- **Procurement / purchase manager** — raises and sends purchase orders,
  manages supplier relationships and negotiated prices.
- **Kitchen / store manager** — records inventory movements (waste, expiry,
  damage, corrections) and watches stock thresholds.
- **Owner / finance** — reads the dashboard KPIs and lifetime spend.

## Core Jobs

1. **Supplier management** — onboard suppliers (business, address, contacts,
   payment terms), track categories, status, and the products each supplies.
2. **Product & stock control** — maintain raw-material products, reorder
   thresholds, cost basis, preferred supplier, and spot low/out-of-stock
   items.
3. **Inventory movements** — record waste/expiry/damage/adjustment/receipt
   transactions that change on-hand stock.
4. **Purchase orders** — draft POs, merge drafts, send to a supplier, and
   track status through the lifecycle.

## Design Principles

- Crimson brand accent on a light content canvas with a dark nav rail.
- Crisp 8px spacing rhythm; clear headline / support / action hierarchy.
- Consistent table styling, tasteful status colors (success/warn/danger/info),
  focus-visible states, hover states, skeletons, empty states, and a small
  toast system.
- Inter typeface, ~10px radius, no gradients-for-decoration, no emoji, no
  nested cards.
